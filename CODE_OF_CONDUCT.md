# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in ytgrab to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel for the project) and to individuals representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the profile at [github.com/bhavukbhardwaj](https://github.com/bhavukbhardwaj), or use GitHub's "Report content" option on the offending comment. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
